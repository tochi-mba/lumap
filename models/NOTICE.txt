lumap hand-tracking models
==========================

This folder holds the two neural-network models lumap uses to follow hands in a camera image.
They are not lumap's own work and are not covered by lumap's MIT OR Apache-2.0 grant: they come
from the projects named below under the Apache License, Version 2.0, and are redistributed
unmodified. lumap runs them with tract, a Rust inference library compiled into lumap itself.

palm_detection_mediapipe_2023feb.onnx
  The MediaPipe palm detector (Google), converted from TensorFlow Lite to ONNX by OpenCV Zoo.
  Source: https://huggingface.co/opencv/palm_detection_mediapipe/resolve/233e619dcea1759bf6de707b9b904fe30881ea55/palm_detection_mediapipe_2023feb.onnx
  SHA-256: 78ff51c38496b7fc8b8ebdb6cc8c1abb02fa6c38427c6848254cdaba57fcce7c
  Licence: Apache License 2.0, per the LICENSE and README files of that repository at that
  revision ("All files in this directory are licensed under Apache 2.0 License").

handpose_estimation_mediapipe_2023feb.onnx
  The MediaPipe hand landmark model (Google), converted from TensorFlow Lite to ONNX by OpenCV Zoo.
  Source: https://huggingface.co/opencv/handpose_estimation_mediapipe/resolve/4b2a0b446e5cf2f11fb6b2c7251091c035d2c1f7/handpose_estimation_mediapipe_2023feb.onnx
  SHA-256: db0898ae717b76b075d9bf563af315b29562e11f8df5027a1ef07b02bef6d81c
  Licence: Apache License 2.0, per the LICENSE and README files of that repository at that
  revision ("All files in this directory are licensed under Apache 2.0 License").
